Solutions

Everything Your Operation Needs

From daily maintenance schedules to compliance reporting, one platform that covers the full lifecycle of your renewable energy assets

01
Scheduled Maintenance

Set up recurring schedules for panel cleaning, turbine inspections, or any routine work. Every task is assigned to the right person, tracked to completion, and never falls through the cracks.

02
Automated Follow-ups

When your team spots an issue during a routine check, the system automatically creates a follow-up task. Whether it's an inverter fault or a blade defect, it's tracked until it's resolved.

03
Visual Site Tracking

See your entire operation at a glance. A real-time overview shows work progress across all your sites so you always know where things stand.

04
Parts & Stock Management

Track spare parts and consumables across every site. Know what's available, what's running low, and where it's needed so your teams always have what they need.

05
Digital Checklists

Pre-built checklists for solar, wind, and hybrid plants guide your technicians through every step. Every response is validated, every completion is time-stamped, and nothing gets missed.

06
Reports & Compliance

Generate professional, export-ready reports whenever you need them. Whether it's a client audit, compliance review, or stakeholder update, the data is always current and ready to share.
