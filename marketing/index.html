Everything Your Operation Needs

From daily maintenance schedules to compliance reporting, one platform that covers the full lifecycle of your renewable energy assets

01
Scheduled Maintenance

Set up recurring schedules for panel cleaning, turbine inspections, or any routine work. Every task is assigned to the right person, tracked to completion, and never falls through the cracks.

02
Automated Follow-ups

When your team spots an issue during a routine check, the system automatically creates a follow-up task. Whether it's an inverter fault or a blade defect, it's tracked until it's resolved.

03
Visual Site Tracking

See your entire operation at a glance. A real-time overview shows work progress across all your sites so you always know where things stand.

04
Parts & Stock Management

Track spare parts and consumables across every site. Know what's available, what's running low, and where it's needed so your teams always have what they need.

05
Digital Checklists

Pre-built checklists for solar, wind, and hybrid plants guide your technicians through every step. Every response is validated, every completion is time-stamped, and nothing gets missed.

06
Reports & Compliance

Generate professional, export-ready reports whenever you need them. Whether it's a client audit, compliance review, or stakeholder update, the data is always current and ready to share.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before getting started

How does the pilot work?

We set up SPHAiR Digital on your actual site so your team can evaluate it with real data. The pilot helps you determine if the platform reduces admin time, improves transparency, and gives you the workforce visibility you need. After the pilot, you choose a plan that fits your operations. You only pay once you've seen the value.

How quickly can we get started?

Most teams are up and running within 30 minutes. SPHAiR Digital comes pre-loaded with industry-standard checklists for solar and wind, so you can start using it immediately without lengthy configuration.

Does it work offline?

Yes. SPHAiR Digital is designed for real-world conditions. Your team can complete checklists and log work offline, and everything syncs automatically when connectivity is restored. Essential for remote solar and wind sites.

What types of renewable energy sites does SPHAiR Digital support?

SPHAiR Digital is built for all renewable energy O&M operations including solar farms, wind parks, hybrid sites, and more. The platform adapts to your specific asset types with configurable checklists, task templates, and reporting.

Can I change plans later?

Absolutely. You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Changes take effect immediately, and billing is prorated so you only pay for what you use.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards and bank transfers. Enterprise customers can also arrange invoicing with net-30 terms.

Is my data secure?

Security is a top priority. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Each organization's data is completely isolated, and we perform regular security audits. Enterprise customers can opt for on-premise deployment for full data control.

Can I customize the platform for my company?

Professional plans include company branding (your logo and colors). Enterprise plans offer full white-label customization and custom integrations with your existing systems.

What kind of support do you offer?

All plans include email support with a 24-hour response time. Professional plans get priority support, and Enterprise customers receive a dedicated account manager with 24/7 availability.

Do you offer annual billing discounts?

Yes. Annual billing saves you up to 20% compared to monthly billing. Use the toggle above to see yearly prices.

Can I cancel at any time?

Yes. There are no long-term contracts. You can cancel your subscription at any time, and you'll retain access until the end of your current billing period.
